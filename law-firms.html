Law Firm Questions

Questions Law Firms Ask

Does cloud AI waive attorney-client privilege?

Not automatically. Opinion 512 lets lawyers use it, but the vetting and the client consent are yours, and every cloud vendor's another review.

Does our firm need dedicated IT staff?

No. We build it with your people and hand over the keys once they can run it, and tiered support's there if you'd rather keep upkeep off your IT desk.
