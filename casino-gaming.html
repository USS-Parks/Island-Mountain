The Compliance Risk

The Cloud AI Problem for Casino Operations

Title 31 of the BSA treats a casino as a financial institution, and Nevada Gaming Commission Regulation 5.260 makes you protect patron data and report cyber incidents within 72 hours. A SAR draft pasted into cloud AI lands on a vendor's servers. On hardware you own it stays on your network, and it'll run air-gapped.

Casino Gaming Questions

Questions Gaming Operations Ask

Does cloud AI create Title 31 compliance risk for casinos?

Yes. Cloud AI puts patron transaction data on a vendor's servers, outside the internal controls 31 CFR Part 1021 requires. On-site, there's no vendor in that chain.

Does our operation need dedicated IT staff?

No. We deploy with your IT team and leave SOPs your surveillance, cage and compliance people can run. Tiered support covers patching if you'd rather not carry it.
