Energy & Utilities Questions

Questions Energy Companies Ask

Does cloud AI complicate NERC CIP compliance?

Yes, though it isn't banned. CIP-004-7 and CIP-011-3 let BCSI sit with a cloud provider under documented controls. The cost is the paperwork and the new conduit.

Does the system connect to SCADA or operational technology?

Not by default. It sits on the IT side and works from what you hand it. If Discovery calls for a read-only feed, we'll scope it with your OT team.
