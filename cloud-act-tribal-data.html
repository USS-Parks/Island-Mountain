Tribal Data Sovereignty

Can the Federal Government Access Tribal Data Stored in the Cloud?

What 18 U.S.C. § 2713 lets federal process reach, why cloud AI fails OCAP® Possession, and how on-premises AI keeps tribal data in tribal hands.

  1. Home
  2. Solutions
  3. CLOUD Act & Tribal Data

Yes. The CLOUD Act (18 U.S.C. § 2713) requires a provider under U.S. jurisdiction to hand over data in its possession, custody or control when it's served with valid federal process, wherever the servers sit. Tribal health, enrollment and governance data on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud is reachable without the nation's consent, and a nondisclosure order under § 2705(b) can keep the nation from hearing about it.

How On-Premises AI Eliminates CLOUD Act Exposure

With the data on tribal hardware, there's no vendor to serve behind the nation's back. A demand has to come to the nation's own door, where tribal counsel sees it and the nation's sovereignty is part of the answer. Our on-premise deployments run open-weight models the nation picks, connected or air-gapped.

What This Means for Tribal IT and Governance Teams

Health, enrollment, child welfare and council records pasted into ChatGPT or Copilot sit with a corporation that'll answer federal process before it tells you. Moving inference on-premises doesn't replace your IT; the system sits in your server room and staff reach it from a browser.

Related Questions

What Tribal Leaders Are Asking

Does the CLOUD Act override tribal sovereignty?

It doesn't address tribal sovereignty at all. The order binds the provider, not the data owner, so the provider complies and the nation may never hear of it.

What are OCAP® principles and why do they matter for tribal AI?

OCAP® (Ownership, Control, Access and Possession) and the CARE principles put tribal data under tribal authority. Cloud AI fails Possession, because the data sits on a corporation's servers and that corporation answers to federal courts, not tribal councils.

Summary: Federal process under 18 U.S.C. § 2713 reaches tribal data on U.S. cloud servers; on-premises AI keeps it where a demand has to come to the nation's door.
