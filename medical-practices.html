The HIPAA Problem

The Cloud AI Problem for Medical Practices

A cloud AI prompt carrying PHI leaves your network, and that vendor becomes your business associate. A "HIPAA-eligible" tier adds a BAA, not control: a breach on their servers can still be reportable under 45 CFR 164.402. On hardware you own, behind your firewall, PHI doesn't leave the building, and it'll run with no internet at all.

Do I need a BAA to use local AI with patient data?

Not with a cloud AI vendor, because there isn't one in the data path. Whether our crew is your business associate gets settled first, in the engagement agreement.

Can non-technical staff use it without IT training?

Yes. They log in from a web browser on the office network, there's nothing to install, and onboarding and SOPs come with the build.
