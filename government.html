The Data Sovereignty Problem

The Cloud AI Problem for Government Agencies

Even a FedRAMP-authorized cloud keeps government data on infrastructure run by private companies and subprocessors. CUI sits under NIST SP 800-171, criminal justice information under CJIS and Federal Tax Information under IRS Publication 1075, and each asks where it's processed and who can reach it. On hardware you own, none of it leaves your network, and it'll run air-gapped.

Government Questions

Questions Government Agencies Ask About Local AI

Can this handle CUI?

Yes, inside an environment that's built for it, along with CJIS and law enforcement sensitive data. It isn't SCIF-rated or certified for classified processing, which runs through different channels.

Does on-premises AI need FedRAMP?

No; FedRAMP's for cloud services. The system joins your existing ATO boundary through your authorizing official's normal change process, with receipts your ISSO pulls into the SSP.
