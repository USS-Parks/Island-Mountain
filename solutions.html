The Common Thread

Every Regulated Industry Shares the Same AI Problem

The frameworks differ, from HIPAA to ITAR to OCAP®, but the problem's the same: cloud AI sends protected data to infrastructure you don't control, under terms that can change. On-premises AI keeps it on your hardware, air-gap capable, and Discovery decides whether that's a few desktops or a rack-mounted estate.
