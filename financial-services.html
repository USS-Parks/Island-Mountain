The Compliance Risk

The Cloud AI Problem for Financial Institutions

Cloud AI puts NPI on a third party's servers. GLBA makes you oversee that vendor under the Interagency Guidelines, NCUA Part 748 or the FTC Safeguards Rule; PCI DSS v4.0.1 pulls it into card-data scope; amended Reg S-P has you contract for its breach notice within 72 hours. On your own hardware NPI stays put, and it'll run air-gapped.
