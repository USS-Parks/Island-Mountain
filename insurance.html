The Cloud AI Problem for Insurance Carriers

A claims file pasted into a cloud AI tool takes health records, driving histories and financials off your network. NAIC Model Law #668, adopted in over 20 states, makes you oversee every vendor touching policyholder data. On a server you own, that data doesn't leave the building, and it'll run air-gapped.

Questions Insurance Organizations Ask About Local AI

Does cloud AI create HIPAA risk for health insurers?

Yes. PHI sent to a cloud AI provider needs a BAA, and even with one it's sitting on servers outside your security controls.

Does our carrier need dedicated IT staff?

No. Your people run it by the SOPs we write with them on-site, and tiered support's there if you'd rather keep upkeep off your IT desk.
