The Compliance Problem

The Cloud AI Problem for Defense Contractors

Send a proposal touching CUI to a cloud AI service and it lands on infrastructure you don't control. DFARS 252.204-7012 wants any cloud holding it at FedRAMP Moderate or equivalent, and most AI APIs don't offer that. On hardware you own, the CUI stays on your network and the system runs air-gapped.

Defense Contractor Questions

Questions Defense Contractors Ask

Does cloud AI create ITAR export risk?

It can. If a provider's infrastructure is reachable by foreign nationals or sits outside the US, processing ITAR technical data may be an unauthorized export under 22 CFR 120-130.

Does this hardware have CMMC certification?

No hardware does. CMMC Phase 2 third-party assessments were suspended on July 13, 2026 pending the program review; Phase 1 self-assessments, NIST SP 800-171 and DFARS 252.204-7012 still apply.
