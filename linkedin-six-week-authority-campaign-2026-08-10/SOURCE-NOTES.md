## Claim boundaries

- A BAA is an important HIPAA contract. It does not replace risk analysis, safeguards, access control, incident response, or other compliance duties.
- Local infrastructure can reduce third-party data exposure. It does not confer regulatory compliance by itself.
- Provider-held data can be subject to provider-directed legal process. Do not claim that every process occurs without notice or consent in every case.
- OCAP® is a framework of the First Nations Information Governance Centre and should not be presented as a universal rule for every tribal nation in the United States.
- The EU proposal describes four sovereignty assurance levels. Describe the official criteria; do not invent a categorical vendor ban.
- Anomaly detection supports AML work. It does not determine suspicious intent and does not replace a risk-based compliance program.
- Nothing in this campaign is legal advice, a compliance certification, or a product specification.
- Basho's supplied bio supports first-person references to tribal emergency-management planning, exercise design, grant strategy, a tribal presidential disaster declaration, a 90-day recovery activation, Type 1 incident public information, amateur radio, and FEMA/CSTI/NWCG training. It does not support invented conversations with unrelated professional roles.
- “The Moving Ceiling” argues that CPG 201's discrete taxonomy, three-year cycle, and point-in-time assessments can't adequately represent adaptive AI-augmented threats. Treat that as the author's thesis, not as an official FEMA conclusion.
- Operation Cascadian Shadow is a designed HSEEP exercise package. Do not claim the exercise has validated outcomes in live delivery unless an after-action record is supplied.
