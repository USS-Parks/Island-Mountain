# Canonical Icon Production Brief

Authority: the complete `icons/` folder, its 108 high-resolution masters, `tools/icon_ingest.py`, and `tools/icon_qa.py`. `BRAND.md` supplies supporting brand context.

## Locked style

- 1254×1254 square PNG master with genuine alpha after chroma removal
- Pure-white monoline technical illustration with rounded stroke ends
- Dense but legible composition, centered, longest art dimension near 85 percent
- Dotted arcs or small dot accents where they support balance
- Every accepted icon must visibly contain the canonical Lamprey Mouth: one solid circular center surrounded by an evenly spaced ring of rounded oval teeth or petals. The supplied canon uses both eight- and ten-tooth forms. The oral disc may be a primary symbol or an embedded seal, control, lens, hub, or node, but it must remain immediately recognizable at 208×208.
- No text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, border, badge, gray, color, fill blocks, gradients, glow, shadow, texture, scene, or photorealism
- Generated against solid `#00FF00`, then converted with the image skill's chroma-removal tool
- Every master receives a 208×208 normalized proof directly in `icons/` through `tools/icon_ingest.py`; every proof must pass `tools/icon_qa.py`
- Existing assets are never overwritten
- No new icon-storage, archive, staging, proof, or rejection folders may be created. Accepted PNG masters belong in `icons/masters`; every other icon artifact belongs directly in `icons/` with a clear filename.
- After every generated candidate, reopen and inspect one established Lamprey Mouth reference from `_icon-sources/webp-originals/` before accepting, processing, or generating the next candidate.
- A candidate without the Lamprey Mouth is a preserved draft, not a canonical master.

## Canonical acceptance evidence

Production was frozen at the owner's direction after icons 01 through 15 were accepted. Each accepted icon has a 1254×1254 transparent PNG master in `icons/masters`, a 208×208 proof directly in `icons`, a clearly visible Lamprey Mouth, and a passing result from `tools/icon_qa.py`.

Icons 16 through 30 remain design briefs only. No master or proof should be inferred from the reserved filenames in the concept table.
