# Campaign Chain Tracking

The chain that matters: clicks, then opt-ins, then calls, then clients. One row per week, filled every Friday. Everything else is vanity.

## Where each number comes from

- **Clicks.** Every post's first comment carries a UTM-tagged link (`utm_campaign=authority-2026`, `utm_content=pNN`). Read them in the islandmountain.io analytics under UTM parameters; `utm_content` attributes the click to the exact post. LinkedIn's own post analytics are secondary color, not the number.
- **Opt-ins.** Cost-worksheet submissions (`/api/worksheet` emails) and Founder's Build Slot claims (`/api/slot`), counted from the notification emails. Posts 01, 08, 25, and 26 link straight at the worksheet gate, so they carry the heaviest opt-in expectation.
- **Calls.** Cal.com scoping calls booked, whatever the route in (chat, voice, contact page). Tag the source in the booking notes when known.
- **Clients.** Signed engagements. Manual entry, Basho's call on attribution.

## Reading it

A post that clicks but never opts in has an audience and no offer; check whether its landing page matches its promise. Opt-ins that never book mean the worksheet email needs a warmer handoff. And a week of silence is data too: change the form mix before changing the message. Ask for a pull anytime and I'll assemble the week's numbers from the analytics and the notification emails.
