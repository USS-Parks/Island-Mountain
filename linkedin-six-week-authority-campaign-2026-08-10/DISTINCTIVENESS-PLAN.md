# Campaign Distinctiveness Plan — APPROVED 2026-08-09, executed STS same day

Policy goal: every one of the 30 article/post pairs is different from the other 29 in form and delivery, and the whole campaign is unmistakable against the AI-written LinkedIn baseline. Nothing executes until this plan is approved. Facts, claim boundaries, sources, and the no-anecdote rule do not change.

## 1. What makes the current set read as one machine

1. **One rhetorical skeleton.** The negation-reframe ("That isn't X. It's Y.") appears 34 times across 29 of 60 pieces. Nearly every article closes on a coined quotable and/or an "Ask X for Y before Z" instruction. Standing feedback already bans section-closing quotables and antithesis titles; three titles are antitheses (05, 14, 22).
2. **One physical format.** Every article is a 330–430 word essay of 10–13 short paragraphs; every feed post is a 110–160 word stanza block with 4 hashtags. The variation checker enforces sentence-level variety inside a single silhouette.
3. **The silhouette is the AI-slop silhouette.** Short stanza lines, flat declarative openers that restate the title, and aphorism closers are the recognized LLM cadence on LinkedIn in 2026. Competent content in that shape gets attributed to a model regardless of who wrote it.

## 2. Policy (the rules, ongoing — not just these 30)

- **R1 — One skeleton, once.** Named devices (ask-closer, quotable-closer, question-open, question-barrage paragraph) are budgeted: max one device instance per piece, no device as the final line, no device in two consecutive publishing days. The negation-reframe ("That isn't X. It's Y.") is banned outright, budget zero, per Basho's 2026-08-09 revision; state the assertion directly ("X is Y, not Z").
- **R2 — Declared form, rotated.** Every post declares a delivery form from the register in §3. No form repeats within a week or on consecutive publishing days. New (post-campaign) posts declare their form the same way.
- **R3 — Pair independence.** A feed post shares no sentence with its article. The feed post is a different angle on the argument, not an excerpt or teaser compression.
- **R4 — Opener diversity.** No two posts in the same week open with the same grammatical shape (declarative thesis, question, condition, instruction, scene, correction of a common claim). The title is never restated as the first line.
- **R5 — Voice floor raised.** Campaign floor moves from 2.5 to 3.0 contractions/100w; first person present in every piece, article and summary. (The voice pass of 2026-08-09 already brought all 60 pieces over 3.0 except a handful of already-passing pieces at 2.5–2.9, which get lifted during reformatting.)
- **R6 — LinkedIn-tell blacklist.** Mechanical ban, added to the slop checker as a new family: "Here's the thing", "Let that sink in", "Read that again", "That's it. That's the post", "Unpopular opinion", "Hot take", "I'll say it again", "Stop doing X. Start doing Y", "gentle reminder", "PSA:", "Agree?", "Thoughts?", engagement-bait questions as closers, emoji bullets, arrow bullets, ♻️/👇 furniture, "A thread".
- **R7 — Titles carry no antithesis.** Retitle 05, 14, 22 (proposals in §3 table).
- **R8 — Hypotheticals stay hypothetical.** Walkthrough-form posts frame scenarios as explicitly imagined ("Picture the delivery day"), never as client stories. The README's no-invented-anecdote rule stands.
- **R9 — The helpful floor.** Every week carries at least two posts whose core is freely usable how-to guidance for anyone deploying on-prem AI: checklists, drills, sizing artifacts, procurement rules the reader can apply without us. No gatekeeping, no pitch in the body. Current mapping: W1 03/04, W2 08/09, W3 13/15, W4 16/19, W5 22/25, W6 26/29.
- **R10 — Voice per Basho's 2026-08-09 revision.** Company practice speaks as "we" in simple present ("We flip the order"); "I" stays for personal conviction, refusals, and lived history. Name the actor whose interest is served instead of leaving agency vague. Colon-fragments become full verb sentences. Word-echoes get clipped with a connective. Ongoing conditions take the progressive ("nobody's measuring").

## 3. Form register and per-post assignment

Eight forms, each grounded in registers Basho already writes in. Each is used 2–5 times across the 30; every week contains five different forms.

- **F1 Field checklist** — the working document itself, annotated. Sparse prose, real line items.
- **F2 Letter to a role** — addressed second-person to the named professional ("To the compliance officer inheriting an AI pilot…").
- **F3 Walkthrough** — a clearly hypothetical scenario run forward in time, narrated.
- **F4 Doctrine clauses** — short numbered commitments/refusals, the way the mission language is written.
- **F5 Objection ladder** — the real pushback quoted plainly, answered one at a time.
- **F6 Spec with commentary** — a mock artifact (load profile, receipt schema, acceptance test list) shown, then annotated.
- **F7 Essay** — the current default, kept for the strongest arguments only.
- **F8 Dissent** — a common industry claim stated fairly, then taken apart.

## 4. Enforcement (Layer 3 — a rule without a gate is a wish)

Extend `qa_content.py` (same file, new checks, wired into the existing pass/fail exit):

- **G1 device budget** — regex counters for the R1 device list; fail on budget breach or device-as-final-line.
- **G2 cross-piece overlap** — no 8-word shingle shared between any two of the 60 pieces (regulation names excepted); catches pair excerpting and template echo.
- **G3 opener shapes** — classify first sentences; enforce R4 per week.
- **G4 form declaration** — each post gains a `**Form:** F3` header line; checker enforces presence, register membership, weekly rotation.
- **G5 one-command gate** — `python qa_content.py --full` runs variation + G1–G4 + voicecheck (floor 3.0) + slopcheck including the new R6 family, and writes `QA-REPORT.md` (currently referenced by the README but missing).

Ongoing: the same rules and gate go into the `im-blog-post` skill source in `_work/skills/` (needs re-upload to claude.ai skill settings to take effect in sessions), and this file's §2 gets a pointer from the campaign README. Anything Basho posts outside this campaign can be run through the same `--full` gate before it ships.
