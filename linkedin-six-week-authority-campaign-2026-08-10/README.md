# Basho Parks LinkedIn Authority Campaign

Publication window: August 10 through September 18, 2026  
Cadence: Monday through Friday, five publications per week  
Package: one long-form article and one shorter LinkedIn post for each of the 30 supplied ideas

## Publishing method

Publish the long-form article first. Use the shorter post as the LinkedIn feed introduction on the same day. Add the article through LinkedIn's native article attachment instead of placing a raw URL in the body copy. Then paste the post's **First comment:** line, UTM link included, as the first comment; that link is how the click chain in `TRACKING.md` gets counted. Attach the matching icon card from the repo-root `cards/` folder as the feed post's image.

The dates below are editorial targets, not automated publication instructions. Every item should receive a final owner read before it goes live.

## Voice and variation controls

- Every post declares a delivery form (F1–F8, assigned in `DISTINCTIVENESS-PLAN.md`). Forms and article opener shapes rotate; no repeats within a week or on consecutive days.
- Sentence lengths, words per sentence, and sentences per paragraph are randomized per piece. The checker requires a meaningful range and standard deviation, rejects three consecutive sentences with the same word count, and no two of the 60 pieces may share a paragraph pattern.
- No sentence repeats anywhere in the campaign, and no 8-word phrase appears in two pieces; feed posts share nothing verbatim with their paired articles.
- Voice floor is 3.0 contractions per 100 words with first person in every piece; "we" carries company practice, "I" carries conviction and refusals. The negation-reframe is banned outright (budget zero), and the LinkedIn-tell blacklist and banned-word list are enforced mechanically.
- Every week carries at least two freely useful how-to posts (checklists, drills, sizing artifacts) a reader can apply without hiring us: W1 03/04, W2 08/09, W3 13/15, W4 16/19, W5 22/25, W6 26/29.
- No invented customer, site, or field anecdotes are allowed; walkthrough scenarios are framed as explicitly hypothetical.
- The supplied customer-count and completed-peer-review claims are not repeated because the project record does not presently substantiate them. “The Moving Ceiling” is described as a paper or manuscript unless publication evidence is added.
- Gate: `python qa_content.py --full` must exit clean before publication. It runs every check above plus the voice and slop checkers and regenerates `QA-REPORT.md`.

## Files

- `WEEK-1.md` through `WEEK-6.md`: publication-ready copy, five article/feed-post pairs per file, each with form and first-comment metadata
- `SOURCE-NOTES.md`: primary sources and claim boundaries
- `DISTINCTIVENESS-PLAN.md`: the approved anti-slop policy, form register, and per-post assignments
- `TRACKING.md`: the clicks, opt-ins, calls, clients ledger and where each number comes from
- `ICON-PROMPTS.md`: the canonical design brief and unique visual concept for every master
- `cards/` (repo root): 2048×2048 icon cards, one per post plus a contact sheet. Solid high-contrast ground per card, white Space Grotesk oblique caps, icon composited from its master at native resolution (never upscaled)
- `make_cards.py`: card generator; colors and title splits live in the table at the top, posts 16–30 get rows when their masters land in `icons/masters/`
- `qa_content.py`: the campaign gate (variation, distinctiveness, voice, slop); run with `--full`
- `QA-REPORT.md`: final verification record, regenerated by each `--full` run
