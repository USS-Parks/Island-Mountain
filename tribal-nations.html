Tribal Government Questions

Questions Tribal Governments Ask

How does local AI protect tribal data sovereignty?

The OCAP® principles, set by the First Nations Information Governance Centre, and the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance put authority over data with the nation. Possession requires your hardware.

Can tribal facilities with limited IT infrastructure support this?

Yes. There's no standard box, so power, space and network get scoped with your IT staff during Discovery, before anything's ordered.
