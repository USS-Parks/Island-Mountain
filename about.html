We Come to You.

Island Mountain AI Inc. is a forward-deployed AI consultancy for organizations that can't send their data to the cloud. We embed on-site, learn the work from its people and build the on-premise system around it.

The Company

Attorney-client communications, patient records, ITAR-controlled data and tribal governance information carry legal obligations cloud AI can't satisfy. So we work on-site, alongside the people the system's meant to help. The hardware's the easy part, and Discovery picks it. Whether a deployment survives depends on learning how you operate, so most of the job is translation and synthesis.
