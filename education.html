The Student Privacy Problem

The Cloud AI Problem for Educational Institutions

FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g) requires institutional control over student records, and COPPA and state laws like SOPIPA restrict what third parties get. A cloud AI vendor is a third party running under its own terms. On hardware you own the records stay on your network, and it'll run air-gapped.

Education Questions

Questions Educators Ask

Does cloud AI create FERPA compliance risk?

It can. The school-official exception needs a vendor under your direct control over records, and proving that control is an audit that never ends. On district hardware, no third party receives them.

Does our institution need additional IT staff?

No. We build it on-site with your people, write the SOPs they'll run it by, and hand over the keys. Tiered support covers upkeep if you'd rather not.
