Before Anyone Names Hardware

How a configuration gets decided

There's no catalog on this page and there isn't one anywhere else either. Nobody can tell you what to buy before knowing what you'd use it for, so the recommendation gets made once, for you, after Discovery. Here's what has to be on the table before it can be made at all.

Whose desk the work runs through

How many people touch it, how often, and whether they're all hitting it at once. Concurrency moves the answer further than anything else on this list, and it's the thing nobody guesses right over the phone.

What the data is, and what it can't touch

Regulated data sets the air-gap posture, and the posture shapes the deployment. A workload that can never see a network is a different build from one that just prefers not to.

What the building can take

Power, cooling, network, and where a rack can physically live. Facilities gets a veto, and it's a great deal cheaper to hear it before anything is ordered than after it arrives.

Then Basho recommends the stack purchase, and we deploy and configure it together, in your building. After that he onboards your team to the workflows and agentic orchestration built for your vertical and for what you sell, because a deployment nobody can run is a very expensive shelf.

The Range

Both ends of this are real answers

A small business doing genuinely useful work on a couple of desktop-class machines and a few laptops sits at one end. A large estate on rack-mounted accelerators with adjacent data server racks, feeding several departments at once, sits at the other. Neither one is the upsell, and there's no ladder between them that anybody gets walked up.

What doesn't change across that spread: it's yours outright, it's air-gap capable, the models are open-weight and swappable, and the Woven Security & Governance Fabric is in the crate either way. When professional accelerators are the right call we'll say so and explain why, and when they're overkill for what Discovery found, we'll say that instead. Every deployment gets built with room to grow, because the alternative is buying twice.

Product Questions

How fast are the answers?

Instant. Inference runs on your own GPUs, so tokens stream back at wire speed - no cloud round-trip, no queue, no latency. Immediate answers, hyper-localized to your rack.

How do you decide what we need?

Discovery decides it. Nobody can tell you what to buy before knowing what you'd use it for, so we sit with the people who run the work until we know it, and the recommendation falls out of that.

Can we start smaller and grow?

Yes, and everything gets built with room to grow, because the alternative is buying twice. You add capacity as the work grows instead of replacing what you already own.
