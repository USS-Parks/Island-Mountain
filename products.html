What We Deploy

A Loosely Connected Band of Pirates

This industry is a loosely connected band of pirates, swapping hardware and weights to suit the moment. We operate the same way and we say so. Depending on what Discovery turns up, a deployment is a bench of DGX Sparks and some M5 laptops, a few RTX 5090s, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwells, or H100 and H200 capacity with adjacent data server racks. The Short-List

Three shapes we keep coming back to

These aren't products and you can't order one. They're the configurations that keep proving themselves, offered as a starting point for the conversation. Discovery decides which shape fits, and plenty of deployments land somewhere between two of them.

The small shop

Two DGX Sparks and a few M5-class laptops. Real work, genuinely useful models, no rack and no dedicated room. For an office where a handful of people carry the institutional knowledge and the data can never leave.

The department

Professional cards in a single chassis, usually RTX 5090s or RTX PRO 6000 Blackwells, serving a floor concurrently. The middle of the range and where most first deployments land, with headroom to add cards rather than start over.

The estate

H100 and H200 capacity with adjacent data server racks, feeding multiple departments and heavier concurrent load. For organizations where AI stopped being a pilot and became infrastructure.

Every one of them is built with room to grow, because the alternative is buying twice. And if you want something not on this list, we'll install that instead. It's your building.

Hardware Integrity

When Professional-Grade Cards Earn Their Keep

Blackwell Architecture

5th-generation Tensor Cores with native FP8 and FP4 and roughly 1 PFLOP of FP16 compute per card, built for modern inference.

96GB GDDR7 per Card

Holds 70B-parameter models in full precision, or DeepSeek V4-Flash at FP8 with headroom for KV cache.

Enterprise Drivers

NVIDIA RTX / Enterprise ISV-certified drivers with a validated, long-lived support lifecycle - not consumer game-ready drivers.

Built for AI Workloads

Large-model fine-tuning, enterprise inference, CAD/CAE, and 24/7 production workloads - the jobs an on-prem stack actually runs.

ECC Error Correction

Detects and corrects single-bit memory errors automatically - no silent data flips in clinical, legal, or defense inference.

~1,792 GB/s Bandwidth

GDDR7 memory feeds the Tensor Cores fast, sustaining high token throughput under continuous, always-on load.

Common Questions

Product Questions

How fast are the answers?

Instant. Inference runs on your own GPUs, so tokens stream back at wire speed - no cloud round-trip, no queue, no latency. Immediate answers, hyper-localized to your rack.

How far does one rack scale?

A single rack ramps to 6-8 RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs. Start with two and add cards as you grow - more VRAM, more throughput, more seats, all on-prem.

How many users can it serve?

A fully ramped rack serves 1,500-1,800 users. One-time hardware, no per-seat cloud fees, no metering. On-prem open source is a no-brainer.
