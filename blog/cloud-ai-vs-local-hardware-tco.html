Cloud AI vs. Local Hardware: Building the Honest Five-Year TCO

Basho Parks

Updated October 28, 2026: Figures are April 2026; cloud rates and RTX PRO 6000 resale values have moved, and the Superlinked post carries a later card price; take the method.

A $500 monthly cloud AI bill doesn't stay $500. Rates climb 10 to 15% a year across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, so by year five a flat $500 is roughly $960. Usage doesn't stay flat; your legal team starts running discovery summaries, you hit the API limits, and overage lands at two to three times unit cost. That's how $500 a month becomes $8,000 of "unexpected" usage.

Compliance Is a Line Item

Handle HIPAA health information or FERPA student records and cloud AI is a compliance operation with its own tab: BAA execution and legal review, annual audits of the cloud AI, retention policy updates, key management, and log monitoring that doesn't stop. That's $15,000 to $30,000 in year one for a covered entity.

Nobody prices the lock-in. Your applications call Azure OpenAI endpoints; moving platforms runs 15 to 25% of annual software spend; OpenAI's changed its API three times since 2023, each a rewrite on the vendor's calendar. On-premises has lock-in too; it's the exit that's different. You can sell the hardware, retire it, or redeploy it, and vLLM speaks a stable, OpenAI-compatible API you update when you're good and ready.

Why the Ownership Side Has No Sticker

A vendor page prints a number here. I'm not going to, and it isn't a negotiating tactic: what gets built comes out of Discovery, shadowing the people who do the work, and I don't know yours yet. An honest ownership column carries the financed payment, power, staff time to keep it patched, and a refresh inside the five years; after the payment ends you're running the same compute with no fee attached.

Run the $500 scenario five years out: $48,700; add 20% for growth and $20,000 for compliance and it's $78,440, gone at the end with nothing on your books. GPU hardware holds residual value; RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell cards resell at 40 to 60% of their original price, and a subscription's is zero.

Cloud wins at low volume and anybody arguing otherwise is selling; once AI's embedded in how you operate, compliance and the exit favor hardware you own, so reach us and we'll work the five-year number on your real usage.
