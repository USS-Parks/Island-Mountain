Bonsai 27B Runs on a Phone. The Sovereignty Math Just Moved Again.

Basho Parks

Somebody put a 27B model in a pocket, a year ahead of the timetable I gave it in June. On July 14 PrismML shipped Bonsai 27B under Apache 2.0, weights on Hugging Face the same day. They took Qwen3.6 27B and rounded every weight down to almost nothing: the ternary build keeps three values per weight and lands at 5.9 GB holding 95% of baseline; the 1-bit build throws out the zero, 3.9 GB, 90% held. The phone claim's literal, roughly four gigabytes resident on an iPhone 17 Pro.

Back in May I called airplane mode the compliance test: if the model answers with the radio off, your data hasn't left. That test runs on a handset now, and the handset can see. A photo read on the device never reaches anybody's API; the adjuster standing in six inches of water drafts the file note right there, and the claim file doesn't enter a third party's building. What goes where in your shop still comes out of Discovery.

Read the Category Table First

That 90% headline's an average, and averages hide things. Math holds 96% of baseline even at 1-bit, because answers you can check survive rounding. The agentic column pays for the compression: tool-calling goes 80.0 to 74.0 ternary, then 66.0 at 1-bit, and vision gives up thirteen points. For a model driving tools through a long loop unsupervised, the 1-bit build isn't ready; bench the ternary. Every retention number here's a vendor claim until somebody independent reruns it.

An agent's needs don't shrink on the way from the rack to the pocket. Identity, egress, metering, audit, a kill switch: the controls that hold still live outside the model, whichever build you run.

American Compression, Chinese Base Model

I've spent real ink asking for an independently funded American open-weight lab. Bonsai is American engineering, shipped Apache 2.0 with the weights on day one. Good on PrismML, genuinely and seriously. It's also built on Qwen3.6, and procurement's provenance conversation doesn't end because the compression happened in Pasadena. The compression work's here; the funded base-model work still isn't.

So pull the ternary build down this week and run it against one workflow you already understand cold, and reach us when you get to the question that hasn't changed in three years of this: whose building the controls sit in.
